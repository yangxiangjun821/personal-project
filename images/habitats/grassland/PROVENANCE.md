The grassland-loss slider draws an illustrative simulation over this photo: overgrazing, farmland, a road and fences, and erosion, the pressures on the edges of the Serengeti ecosystem. The plains in the photo lie inside the national park; the picture is not a record of what has happened there.

The descent to this photo streams Sentinel-2 cloudless 2016 satellite tiles and Terrain Tiles on AWS Open Data elevation at runtime; see `images/habitats/forest/PROVENANCE.md` for those credits. Clouds and atmosphere in the dive are procedural.
