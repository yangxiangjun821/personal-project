The reef-loss slider draws an illustrative simulation over this photo: a marine heatwave, coral bleaching, algae overgrowth and rubble, the usual course of a mass bleaching event. Western Australia had such a heatwave in 2011. The picture is not a record of the reef's condition.

The descent to this photo streams Sentinel-2 cloudless 2016 satellite tiles and Terrain Tiles on AWS Open Data elevation at runtime; see `images/habitats/forest/PROVENANCE.md` for those credits. Clouds and atmosphere in the dive are procedural.
