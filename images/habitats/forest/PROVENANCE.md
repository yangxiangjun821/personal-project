The forest-loss slider (`forest-loss.js`) draws an illustrative simulation over this photo: logging roads, clear-cutting, burning and oil palm, the usual sequence of deforestation in Borneo. It is not a record of what has happened in the Danum Valley, which is a protected conservation area.

The descent between the globe and this photo streams Sentinel-2 cloudless 2016 satellite tiles at runtime:
"Sentinel-2 cloudless – https://s2maps.eu by EOX IT Services GmbH (Contains modified Copernicus Sentinel data 2016)", CC BY 4.0.

The dive also streams real elevation for its 3D terrain from Terrain Tiles on AWS Open Data (Mapzen terrarium encoding; SRTM, GMTED2010, ETOPO1 and others). Clouds and atmosphere in the dive are procedural.
