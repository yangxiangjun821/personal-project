The shore-pollution slider draws an illustrative simulation over this photo: plastic washing up, an oil spill on the water and oil on the tide line. The stage texts refer to the 2001 Jessica spill off San Cristóbal; Punta Espinosa itself is not known to have been oiled.

The descent to this photo streams Sentinel-2 cloudless 2016 satellite tiles and Terrain Tiles on AWS Open Data elevation at runtime; see `images/habitats/forest/PROVENANCE.md` for those credits. Clouds and atmosphere in the dive are procedural.
