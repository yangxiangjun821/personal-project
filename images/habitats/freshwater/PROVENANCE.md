The wetland-loss slider draws an illustrative simulation over this photo: less floodwater arriving, drought, fire and a dry plain. Upstream dams and irrigation in Angola and Namibia have been proposed, not built; the picture is not a record of what has happened in the delta.

The descent to this photo streams Sentinel-2 cloudless 2016 satellite tiles and Terrain Tiles on AWS Open Data elevation at runtime; see `images/habitats/forest/PROVENANCE.md` for those credits. Clouds and atmosphere in the dive are procedural.
