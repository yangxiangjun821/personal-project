# Image credits

The animal photographs are real photographs sourced from Wikimedia Commons. Each image licence and artist credit is recorded with its source.

The complete machine-readable source, artist, and licence record for the 100-animal field guide is stored in `images/animal-photo-credits.json`. The Antarctic blue whale card shares the credited blue whale photograph.

- The human-impact sliders progressively reveal AI-edited adaptations of these photographs (`pollution-scenes.js`, `pollution-renderer.js`). The original files remain unchanged. Adaptations and exact prompts are in `images/habitats/human-impact/PROVENANCE.md`; adaptations retain the corresponding source licence, including ShareAlike. They illustrate hypothetical damage and do not document events at the named sites.
- Satellite imagery for the dive (all five habitats) — Sentinel-2 cloudless 2016, https://s2maps.eu by EOX IT Services GmbH (contains modified Copernicus Sentinel data 2016), CC BY 4.0. Tiles are streamed at runtime. Details in `images/habitats/forest/PROVENANCE.md`.
- Elevation for the dive's 3D terrain — Terrain Tiles on AWS Open Data (Mapzen terrarium encoding), https://registry.opendata.aws/terrain-tiles/; sources include SRTM, GMTED2010 and ETOPO1. Streamed at runtime.
- Clouds, atmosphere and stars in the dive are rendered procedurally; they are illustrative, not weather data.
